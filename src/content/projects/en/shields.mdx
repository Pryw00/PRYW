Shields is a serious game designed to simulate cybersecurity incidents and build a culture of security in companies and institutions.

Built entirely in Unity with a custom backend, the game drops players into crisis scenarios where they must identify threats, choose mitigations and protect digital assets against the clock. The game mechanics make good security practices easier to absorb — and to remember.
