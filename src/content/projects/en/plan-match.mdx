An AlphaZ Foundation initiative that lets Ecuadorian citizens compare the 2025–2029 presidential candidates' government plans point by point.

The tool organizes proposals into themes (jobs, education, security, health, the economy and more) and presents comparative visualizations that support informed decisions during the election.
