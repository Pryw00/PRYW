A web tool built for the Municipal Government of San Miguel de Ibarra that lets citizens and officials check name availability by sector within the city's cadastral system.

The app integrates with Ibarra's SIGM (Municipal Information and Management System), providing real-time lookups and simplifying procedures that used to require an in-person visit.
