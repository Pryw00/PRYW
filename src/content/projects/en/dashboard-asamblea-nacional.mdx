An interactive, retro-gaming style dashboard built for Ecuador's National Assembly. The platform makes it visual and intuitive to explore information about Assembly members: profiles, voting history, contact details and legislative statistics.

The project covered full-stack architecture, a user interface with its own visual identity, and ongoing technical support for the internal team.
